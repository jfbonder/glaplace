## Corrections you MUST apply relative to the earlier note/README (found by the librarians)
1. Lieberman 1991: g depends on t only; x-dependence only as a Hölder perturbation of size (1+g(|ξ|))|x−y|^α; double phase and p(x) are NOT covered; needs bounded solution and C^{1,α} boundary/data; the boundary part of his Thm 1.7 is attested only via other papers (check status in REFERENCES_A). Cianchi–Maz'ya: convex domains, f∈L^{n,1}, Orlicz-type x-independent operators: ∇u∈L^∞(Ω). p(x): Fan 2007 JDE (C^{1,β} boundary, p Hölder on closed domain) — status MEM. Double phase: interior results only (Colombo–Mingione 2015a,b with strict q/p<1+α/n resp. bounded minimisers and q≤p+α; Baroni–Colombo–Mingione 2018 for the endpoint; De Filippis–Mingione 2021; Hästö–Ok 2022); (S) up to the boundary must be assumed there; mention the unrefereed preprint (Missaoui arXiv 2601.11495) only in TODO_VERIFY, not as a basis.
2. Strong convergence "saddle point + closed range ⇒ strong convergence" is FALSE in general in infinite dimensions: ADMM with ρ=r is Douglas–Rachford applied to the dual, which converges weakly in general (Eckstein–Bertsekas, Svaiter, Bauschke–Combettes Thm 26.11) and strong convergence can fail (Bùi–Combettes 2020 counterexample for Douglas–Rachford). Our Theorem A is the standard weak-convergence statement; strong convergence needs uniform monotonicity (Thm B).
3. Glowinski Ch. VI Thm 5.1 (per Glowinski–Pan–Tai 2016, the best accessible reproduction): V,H Hilbert; F,G proper convex lsc; saddle point; B injective with closed range; F(q)/|q|→∞; F=F0+F1 with F0 Gâteaux differentiable and uniformly convex on bounded sets; 0<ρ<(1+√5)r/2; conclusion (u_n,ν_n)→(u,Bu) strongly, multiplier only weakly. The uniform convexity on bounded sets of F(q)=∫|q|^p/p in H=L^2 holds for p≥2 only: THIS is why the old theorem required p≥2 (Aragón–Fernández Bonder–Rubio applied Thm 5.1 for p≥2 in H^1_0×L^2). Flag in TODO_VERIFY that the exact hypotheses/numbering must be checked against the book. Note that our Theorem B(b) obtains strong convergence of ν_n in L^p (not L^2) for 1<p<2 under (S).
4. Lavrentiev: BDS 2020 Thm 32: gap for double phase when q > p + α·max{1,(p−1)/(d−1)} (see REFERENCES_A for the exact statement and any extra hypotheses); BGS 2022: no gap if q ≤ p + α·max{1,p/d} (sharp for p≤d); for p(x) just below log-Hölder there are gaps (BDS Thm 30, Zhikov). Carstensen–Ortner 2010 and Balci–Ortner–Storn 2022: conforming FEM fails exactly when a gap is present; conforming discrete energies converge to the infimum over smooth functions, Crouzeix–Raviart elements to the true minimum.
5. ELM 2004: sharpness of q/p<(n+α)/n shown in the range p<n<n+α<q and the 'regularity' they prove is higher integrability (Du∈L^q_loc), not C^{1,β}; FMM 2004: W^{1,p} minimiser with singular set of dimension >N−p−ε, for 1<p<N<N+α<q, discontinuous.
6. In the examples: the two-phase experiments use a = indicator function (not Hölder) → outside the regularity theory for (S) and outside the Hölder hypothesis of the no-gap results; radial two-phase has an exact solution and the observed order was 1.90 (L²) and 0.79 (sup): say so. e^t−1−t and e^t−1 are outside Lieberman (no Δ2) — (S) not available; observed behaviour only. Never claim convergence of the continuous algorithm there.
7. The repo README contains the statement "Standard ADMM theory gives convergence...": in the paper that is Theorem C with a proof.
